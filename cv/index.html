Projects

  • Aug 2026 - present
    NEGSC Reimplementation and Audit

    Modular public reimplementation and code audit of NEGSC (2024), a self-supervised graph-contrastive NIDS method (NEGAT encoder with Wasserstein / Gromov-Wasserstein losses) on NF-BoT-IoT-v2 NetFlow data. The associated extension work is private and incomplete; no final method or result is claimed.

    • Analysed the paper and reference notebooks; reconstructed the pipeline as modular Python code
    • Documented six paper-vs-code discrepancies (dead hyperparameters, untrained per-step edge embedder, ndata key mismatch, and others)
    • Public foundation for ongoing private NIDS extension research
  • Jun 2026 - Aug 2026
    Anomal-E Analysis and Modular Reimplementation

    Modular implementation and audit of Anomal-E, an edge-centric self-supervised GNN method (E-GraphSAGE + DGI) for network anomaly detection on NetFlow data.

    • Analysed the paper and restructured the implementation into modular components (data pipeline, graph builder, encoder, DGI, detectors, trainer)
    • Investigated preprocessing and target-encoding leakage concerns during the audit
    • Vectorised graph construction (NetworkX to DGL/NumPy) to eliminate OOM issues on full datasets
  • Jul 2026 - Aug 2026
    SL-GAD Reimplementation and Study

    Reimplementation of SL-GAD (TKDE 2021), a node-centric self-supervised graph anomaly-detection method combining generative attribute regression with multi-view contrastive learning.

    • Implemented the full training and multi-round evaluation pipeline with a CLI
    • Single-run AUC of 0.9167 on Cora, within the paper’s reported range (not a full reproduction study)
    • Studied contrastive/generative ideas relevant to the broader NIDS direction; recognised the node-centric vs flow-level distinction
