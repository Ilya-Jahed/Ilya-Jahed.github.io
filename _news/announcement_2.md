I've started working on auditing and extending the **NEGSC (2024)** self-supervised graph-based NIDS framework as part of my research at the [Scalable Systems Lab](https://ssl.iust.ac.ir/) at IUST.

The active extension work is private and incomplete; no final method or results are claimed yet. The public repositories below document my reimplementation and audit work and serve as the foundation:

- [NEGSC reimplementation and audit](https://github.com/Ilya-Jahed/negsc-fraud-detection)
- [SL-GAD](https://github.com/Ilya-Jahed/sl-gad-reimplementation) — node-centric graph anomaly detection baseline
- [Anomal-E](https://github.com/Ilya-Jahed/Anomal-E-Implementation) — edge-centric flow anomaly detection
